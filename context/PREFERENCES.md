# Preferences

These override [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) when they conflict. Edit them freely; they
should change as you learn what you like.

## Writing

- Spelling, e.g. American or British English.
- Tone, e.g. plain and direct, no filler.

## Working style

- Ask me instead of guessing when something is unclear.
- Show me a plan before a large change.

## Pull requests

- Whether an AI may merge its own memex PR once CI passes (default: no, I merge).
- Whether a dream PR may merge itself when no question is open (default: no).

## Privacy

- Topics that stay out of the memex entirely, e.g. medical or financial records.
- Topics that may be stored but never leave it.
